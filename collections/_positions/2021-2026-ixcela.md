<section class="job-description">
    <h2>Job Description</h2>
    [Ixcela](https://ixcela.com/) uses blood metabolite tests to measure gut microbiome signals to make dietary and lifestyle recommendations that either help patients meet their goals or support them through illness.
    A former coworker had joined Ixcela and recommended me for a role as they were increasing their development team. Initially, we were there to modernize their collection of scripts that communicated with their [LIMS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laboratory_information_management_system). We transformed these scripts into an actual backend web API. Later, I took over as the sole maintainer. I then integrated FedEx to automate printing shipping labels for our blood test kits. After that I added shipment tracking to the system to help us see when completed kits would be returning.
    We eventually transitioned to using [Practice Better](https://practicebetter.io/) as our patient-facing system, at which point I integrated it with our other systems in order to help maintain our own internal records. Additionally, we wanted to combine all of this data from our various systems to help us track how the business was doing, so I created dashboards to help us track important metrics.
